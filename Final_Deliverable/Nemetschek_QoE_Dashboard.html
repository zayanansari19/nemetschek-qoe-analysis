Analysis Methodology

1. Framework

This analysis follows a Big 4 Transaction Services Quality of Earnings (QoE) framework adapted for a software/SaaS company undergoing a subscription transition. The methodology evaluates the sustainability, predictability, and quality of Nemetschek's earnings through ten analytical dimensions.

2. Data Sources

  • Primary: Nemetschek SE Annual Report 2024 (consolidated financial statements, notes, management report)
  • Cross-reference: Annual Reports 2020-2023 for historical data and trend verification
  • Supplementary: H1 2025 Report for forward indicators; Company Presentation 2024 for strategic context
  • All data is publicly available and sourced from official Nemetschek SE publications

3. Revenue Quality Assessment

Revenue is decomposed into four streams: Subscription/SaaS, Maintenance, Perpetual Software Licenses, and Consulting & Hardware. The key quality metric is the share and growth trajectory of recurring revenue (Subscription + Maintenance), which provides contracted, predictable cash flows. The subscription transition is tracked through the Sub/SaaS-to-Recurring ratio and the decline rate of perpetual licenses.

4. ARR Analysis

Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) is the annualized run-rate of subscription and maintenance contracts at period end. An ARR/Revenue ratio above 1.0x indicates embedded forward growth (new contracts signed during the year contribute run-rate beyond what was recognized). Organic ARR growth (excluding acquisitions) isolates the underlying business momentum from M&A-driven expansion.

5. Organic vs Inorganic Growth

Revenue growth is decomposed into organic (same-perimeter, constant currency) and inorganic (M&A contribution). For FY2024, GoCanvas (acquired July 1, 2024) contributed €27.5M in 6 months of post-acquisition revenue. Currency-adjusted figures normalize for EUR/USD fluctuations that affect reported growth given significant Americas exposure (~40% of revenue).

6. Segment Profitability

Each of the four segments (Design, Build, Manage, Media) is analyzed for revenue contribution, growth rate, EBITDA margin, and margin trend. This identifies which segments drive group performance and which carry risk. The Build segment's margin decline is directly attributable to GoCanvas integration, while Design's margin expansion reflects operating leverage from the subscription transition.

7. Cash Conversion

Two cash conversion metrics are tracked: Operating Cash Flow / Net Income (measures quality of accrual earnings) and Free Cash Flow before M&A / Net Income (measures underlying cash generation capacity). A ratio consistently above 100% indicates earnings are backed by cash — a hallmark of high-quality software businesses with upfront annual billing and low working capital needs.

8. Working Capital & DSO

Days Sales Outstanding (DSO = Trade Receivables / Revenue × 365) tracks collection efficiency. The 11.3-day increase in FY24 is flagged as it outpaces revenue growth, potentially indicating extended payment terms or GoCanvas receivables integration. Deferred revenue (contract liabilities) is analyzed as a forward visibility indicator — growing deferred revenue signals increasing pre-paid commitments.

9. Balance Sheet Assessment

The GoCanvas acquisition fundamentally changed Nemetschek's balance sheet: from net cash (€268M cash, ~€0 debt) to net debt (~€295M). Goodwill increased from €552M to €1,135M, representing 53% of total assets. The equity ratio dropped from 61% to 44%. These changes are assessed for impact on financial flexibility and future M&A capacity.

10. Risk Rating Methodology

  • LOW: Metric supports the investment thesis; no material concern. Typical for strong recurring revenue, good cash conversion, geographic diversification.
  • MEDIUM: Metric requires monitoring; potential for deterioration. Applied to balance sheet leverage, DSO trend, and weaker segments.
  • HIGH: Metric represents material risk to the thesis. Reserved for the GoCanvas integration given the size of the acquisition, high goodwill, and low initial margins.

11. Limitations

  • Analysis based solely on publicly available annual report data — no access to management or internal metrics
  • FY2020-2022 segment data in absolute EUR is estimated from reported percentage splits where note-level data was not available
  • Net revenue retention, customer-level churn, and contract duration data are not publicly disclosed
  • GoCanvas integration is assessed on only 6 months of post-acquisition data
  • Currency-adjusted figures use company-reported constant-currency growth rates, not independently calculated rates
